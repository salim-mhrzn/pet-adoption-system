About Pet Connect

Connecting loving families with pets who need a forever home.

🐾 Our Mission

At Pet Connect, our mission is to simplify and modernize the pet adoption process. We provide a trusted and secure platform where adopters can easily explore, connect, and adopt pets in need of loving homes.

🌎 Our Vision

Our vision is to reduce the number of homeless pets by leveraging technology to make adoption transparent, accessible, and efficient for everyone.

Adoption Rules & Regulations 🐶🐱

Please read and follow these rules to ensure a smooth and safe adoption process.

1. Age Requirement

Only individuals above 18 years can adopt pets and must provide valid ID.

2. Approval Required

Adoption requests are subject to review and approval by Pet Connect staff.

3. Home Visits

Home visits may be conducted for certain pets to ensure proper care and environment.

4. Proper Care

Adopters must provide shelter, food to the pet and Pets must not be sold, traded, or abandoned after adoption.

5. Vaccinations

All pets are provided with basic vaccinations and deworming; adopters should follow up with a veterinarian.

6. Agreement

By adopting, you agree to abide by all rules. Pet Connect reserves the right to follow up on the pet's welfare.
